03 / Behind the brush

Hi, I’m Cristie.

I grew up in a big family where everyone had their own thing. I found mine junior year when I went to nail school at JATC. A Michael Jackson–themed nail art set took me to first place in Utah and ninth at nationals in Georgia.

I got licensed and started taking clients that same year. Later, I put my nail career on hold to serve God and others on an LDS mission. Stepping away meant trusting that the talent I’d discovered would still be there when I came home.

Now I’m home from my mission and ready to keep serving others through the work I love. I love color, nature, sports and art, and one of my favorite things about doing nails is helping people express a little more of who they are.
